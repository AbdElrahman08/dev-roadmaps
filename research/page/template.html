Free dev roadmaps as a business

Evidence collected so far on turning the verified-free roadmaps into a business. Every record links to its source and the date it was read. Unknown values stay blank. None of them has been estimated.

Licence finding · RK-001 / D-001 (decided: rebuild independently)

The roadmap.sh licence allows personal use only. It forbids publishing the content "in any form" outside the repository without prior consent from the author. Decided on 6 Nov 2026: the public site, Darb, is rebuilt independently, and the data derived from roadmap.sh stays private. This isn't legal advice. Talk to a lawyer before you decide.

What we know

  • Competitors charge roughly $15–40 a month, or $239–399 a year (Codecademy, Coursera Plus, Boot.dev).
  • roadmap.sh already sells Team seats at $10 a seat per month and AI features to the same audience.
  • Fully free curricula run on donations (freeCodeCamp is a US 501(c)(3); The Odin Project also takes donations).
  • Class Central funds course discovery with ads and affiliate links.
  • Egypt's government funds free tech training at scale (Digital Pioneers aims at 12,000 a year; DEPI has 3,000+ graduates through one vendor).

What we don't know

  • Demand: there are no interviews, survey responses or usage data yet.
  • TAM, SAM, SOM, CAC, churn and our own price are all unknown.
  • Whether the roadmap.sh author would consent, and on what terms.
  • Individual roadmap.sh Pro prices and Scrimba prices (neither page showed them).
  • Search demand and traffic (Semrush has no API units left).
